# Synthesis and Ranked Output

Load this after all five dimensions are covered.

---

## Step 1 — Reflect the pattern back

Before ranking, show the founder what came up across the five dimensions:

> "Here's what came up across the five dimensions:
>
> **Pain intensity:** [which job scored strongest + evidence noted]
> **Broken workarounds:** [which job has the biggest opportunity gap]
> **Strategic fit:** [which job unlocks the most]
> **Founder edge:** [where the asymmetric advantage sits]
> **Evidence quality:** [which job has the most grounded support]
>
> Based on that: here's the ranking."

---

## Step 2 — Propose the ranking

Present the ranked list in this format:

> **#1 — [Job statement]**
> [2–3 sentence rationale grounded in the five dimensions]
> Watch out for: [honest risk or unproven assumption]
>
> **#2 — [Job statement]**
> [2–3 sentence rationale]
> Watch out for: [honest risk or unproven assumption]
>
> [continue for all jobs]
>
> Does this ranking reflect what you know, or does something feel off?

If the founder pushes back on the ranking, ask what specifically feels wrong and
adjust with reasoning — do not simply accept a reorder without probing why.

---

## Step 3 — Confirm

Wait for confirmation before proceeding to Kill-or-keep.

---

## Guardrail responses for split or ambiguous signals

**If all five dimensions point to different jobs:**
> "Your five dimensions are pointing in different directions — that's actually useful
> information. Pain intensity and evidence quality are the two that matter most at
> your stage. Let those two break the tie."

**If the founder wants to pursue two jobs simultaneously:**
> "Two jobs means two products. At your stage, that's a resource split you probably
> can't afford. Which one has the customer in more pain today? Start there. You can
> revisit the second job after your first customer validation."

**If the founder has very thin evidence across all jobs:**
> "Your evidence is thin across the board — and that changes how much confidence to put
> in any ranking. The right next step may be validation before prioritization. Which job
> is cheapest to test in the next two weeks?"

---
