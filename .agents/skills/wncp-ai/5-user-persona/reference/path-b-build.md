# Path B — Build from Scratch

Load this when the founder selects "Not yet."

Three questions. One at a time. Do not move to the next question until the current one passes.
The founder's product stays out until Synthesis.

---

## Question 1 — The Person

**Goal:** Name one specific person — role, company type or context, what they are responsible
for. Not a segment. Not a demographic. One person you could call.

**Ask:**

> "The person.
>
> [If value-prop found:] Your value prop pointed to [In Scope type]. Zoom in on that.
> Who specifically is this person — what is their role, what kind of company or context
> are they in, and what are they responsible for?
>
> [If value-prop not found:] Who is the one person your product is most urgently for?
> Give me their role, the kind of company or context they are in, and what they are
> responsible for."

If discovery data shows clear customer signals, anchor it: "Your discovery interviews show
you spoke with [X type] and [Y type] — which one described the problem most urgently?"

**What to do with their answer:**

| Answer type | Response |
|-------------|----------|
| Specific role + company context | Accept — move to Question 2 |
| Demographic segment ("SMBs", "millennials", "busy professionals") | "That's a category. Give me one person within it — what is their title? What do they have responsibility for?" |
| Role without context ("CTOs", "founders") | "A [role] at what kind of company? At what stage? The context is what makes the problem land differently for them." |
| Two or more person types named | **Hard stop.** "You've named two people. We are defining one persona — we cannot move forward with two. Which one has the more urgent version of this problem? Pick one and we'll set the other aside." Do not move forward until they commit to one. |
| Aspirational customer ("someone who would really benefit") | "That's who you hope uses it. Who is most likely to have this problem right now, today? Give me a real person — role and context." |

Do not move forward until you have one specific role in one specific context.

---

## Question 2 — The Situation

**Goal:** Name the specific moment the problem surfaces — a task, a decision point, an event.
Not a general description of their role. Not a state ("overwhelmed", "frustrated"). A moment.

**Ask:**

> "The situation.
>
> Describe what this person is doing when the problem hits them. Give me the moment —
> a task they're in the middle of, a decision they're trying to make, something concrete
> that's happening in their day or week."

**What to do with their answer:**

| Answer type | Response |
|-------------|----------|
| Specific task, decision, or event | Accept — move to Question 3 |
| General state ("they're overwhelmed", "they're always behind") | "That's a state, not a moment. What are they doing — what task or decision are they in the middle of when this surfaces?" |
| Product context ("when they open the app", "when they're using a tool") | "Set the product aside. What's happening in their work or life before they reach for any tool?" |
| Multiple situations named | "You've named a few moments. Which one is the most painful — the one where not solving it costs them the most?" |
| Role description repeated | "That's describing who they are again. I need the moment — what are they doing, specifically, when the problem comes up?" |

Do not move forward until you have a concrete moment.

---

## Question 3 — The Need

**Goal:** Name the one thing this person most needs to accomplish in that moment —
solution-agnostic. Not what your product does. Not a feature. What *they* need done.

**Ask:**

> "The need.
>
> In that moment you just described, what is the one thing this person most needs to
> get done? Not what your product does — what do *they* need to accomplish, regardless
> of how they do it?"

**What to do with their answer:**

| Answer type | Response |
|-------------|----------|
| Solution-agnostic need, one thing | Accept — move to Synthesis |
| Product feature request ("they need an app that...", "they need a dashboard") | "That's describing a solution. Strip the product out — what are they trying to accomplish regardless of how?" |
| JTBD-format statement ("When I... I want to... so I can...") | "That's a job statement — great instinct, but we write that in the next exercise. For now, one short phrase: what do they need done in that moment?" |
| Multiple needs | "You've named more than one. Which is highest stakes — the one that, if left undone, costs them the most?" |
| Vague outcome ("get things done faster", "be more productive") | "More specific — what specifically do they need done? What task, what decision, what result?" |

Do not move to Synthesis until you have one solution-agnostic need.

---

## Synthesis

Once all three answers are in, reflect them back:

> "You have all three pieces:
>
> - The person: [their answer]
> - The situation: [their answer]
> - The need: [their answer]"

Then ask one question:

> "Why this person over other plausible users? If you have discovery data, point to the
> signal — which interviews or patterns pointed here? If not, what is your best read on
> why this ICP has the most urgent version of this problem compared to others you could
> have chosen?"

**What to do with their answer:**

| Answer type | Response |
|-------------|----------|
| Evidence from discovery data or reasoned comparison | Accept — move to Validation |
| "I don't know" or "just a guess" | "That's fine at this stage. What is the most compelling reason you have, even if it's a hypothesis? We'll note it as such." |
| Repeats the persona description instead of explaining the choice | "That's describing the persona again. The question is: why this person over other plausible customers? What makes their version of the problem more urgent?" |

---

## Validation

Run all three criteria. Do not accept partial passes. Do not proceed to Closing until all pass.

| Criterion | Pass | Fail → one probe |
|-----------|------|------------------|
| **Single person, not a segment** | One specific role in one specific context — not a category, not a range, not two types | "Still a segment. Give me one person — role and company context, nothing broader." |
| **Specific moment, not a general state** | A concrete task, decision, or event that is happening | "Still a state. What are they doing — give me the moment, not the condition." |
| **Solution-agnostic need** | No product reference, no feature description, no tool named | "Your product is in there. Strip it out — what does the person need to accomplish, independent of any solution?" |

**If one criterion fails:** name exactly what is wrong and return one probe. Do not rewrite
the persona fields. Ask the founder to revise. After revision, re-run all three before proceeding.

**If all three pass:** go to Closing in the SKILL.
