# Path A — Pressure Test an Existing Persona

Load this when the founder selects "I already have a user persona."

The founder has already shared their persona. Do two things before responding.

## Step 1 — Check against the value prop

Compare their persona's person against the In Scope customer from
`2_Outputs/.agents/A.3-Value-Prop/Value-Proposition.md`.

If they conflict, surface it immediately before running the criteria:

> "Your value prop targets [In Scope person]. Your persona describes [their person]. These
> don't match — which one does your product actually serve? We should align before moving
> forward."

Do not proceed until they reconcile the two.

## Step 2 — Run the three validation criteria

| Criterion | Pass | Fail → one probe |
|-----------|------|------------------|
| **Single person, not a segment** | One specific role in one specific context | "That's a category. Give me one person within it — what is their title and what kind of company are they in?" |
| **Specific moment, not a general state** | A concrete task, decision, or event | "That's a state, not a moment. What are they doing — give me the specific task or decision when the problem surfaces?" |
| **Solution-agnostic need** | No product reference, no feature description | "Your product is in there. Strip it out — what does the person need to accomplish, independent of any solution?" |

- **If all three pass:** "Your persona holds up — let's save it." Go to Closing in the SKILL.
- **If one or more fail:** Name exactly what fails and return one probe per failure. Address
  the most fundamental failure first (person → situation → need, in that order). Do not
  rewrite their persona — probe only.

**If the founder won't revise after a failed criterion:**

> "I can't save a persona that [names the specific failure]. We need to fix that before
> moving on — otherwise the JTBD exercise will be built on a shaky foundation. What would
> you change?"

If they still won't revise, stop. Do not save an invalid persona.

After any revision, re-run all three criteria before proceeding to Closing.
