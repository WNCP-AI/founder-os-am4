# Mode B — Sharpen

Load this when the founder already has a value proposition. Your job is to make it more specific
and concise — not rewrite it for them. Use the discovery data to ground every question. If their
statement contradicts what customers actually said, name the specific evidence.

Start with: "I have a few questions to help make your value proposition more specific — let's go through them one at a time."

**Evaluation gate (Mode B)** — run internally; ask one question per failed criterion, one at a
time, waiting for the answer before the next. As each is resolved, have them update that section
of their template.

| Criterion | Pass | Fail → ask this (one at a time) |
|---|---|---|
| **Customer is specific** | Names a person or situation | "Who specifically has this problem? Can you describe one person from your interviews?" |
| **Problem is real and grounded** | Matches something customers said | "Your discovery data shows [specific signal] — is that the problem you're solving, or something different?" |
| **Alternative is named** | Names what they do today | "What do they do today if your product doesn't exist? What does that look like specifically?" |
| **Differentiator is defensible** | Matches a gap customers named | "Your data shows [specific gap] — does your differentiator connect to that?" |

Work through every failed criterion. One question. Wait for the answer. Next question. When all
criteria pass (or have been addressed), go to Closing in the SKILL — don't let this become a
rewrite session.
