# Path B — Working from Existing Hypotheses

Load this when the founder already has hypotheses they want to clean up and rank.

---

## Step 1 — Clean up before you rank

Once the founder has pasted their hypotheses, do not proceed straight to ranking. First
reformat every hypothesis into the standard format. Most raw founder hypotheses fall into
one of these traps:

| Problem type | Example | Rewrite to |
|---|---|---|
| Solution statement | "We believe our app will help users manage tasks better" | "We believe users currently struggle to [specific task] and that this costs them [specific outcome]" |
| Too vague | "Customers have problems with X" | "We believe [specific customer type] experiences [specific friction] when [specific situation]" |
| Circular | "We believe customers need our product" | Discard and replace with the underlying assumption about the problem or customer |
| Opinion stated as fact | "The market wants faster turnaround" | "We believe [customer type] prioritizes speed over accuracy in [situation]. Evidence: [none / weak / strong]" |

Show the cleaned list back to the founder:
> "Here's how I've reframed your hypotheses — does this still capture what you meant?
> Let me know if anything feels off before we rank."

Wait for their confirmation. If they want to adjust anything, do it one item at a time.

---

## Step 2 — Fill the gaps

After the founder confirms the cleaned list, internally check which of the three categories
are thin or missing. If any are, ask:
> "I notice you haven't named any assumptions about [Customer / Problem / Solution].
> That's often where the biggest surprises come from — want to spend two minutes
> on that before we rank?"

If they say yes, draw out 1–2 hypotheses for that category using the relevant questions from
`reference/path-a-scratch.md` (Customer, Problem, or Solution section as appropriate).
One question at a time.

If they say no, note the gap and proceed. Flag missing categories explicitly during ranking:
"This list has no Customer hypotheses — that's a blind spot worth naming."

---

## Step 3 — Confirm the stack

When you have 6–8 hypotheses across all three categories and the founder has confirmed the
cleaned list, check the count:

- **6–8 total:** proceed to ranking.
- **More than 8:** "We have [N] — that's more than an interview guide can hold. Which ones
  feel like duplicates or lower stakes? Let's consolidate to 6–8 before we rank."
- **Fewer than 6:** draw out additional hypotheses using questions from the relevant category
  sections in `reference/path-a-scratch.md`.

When the count and list are confirmed, proceed to Ranking. Load `reference/ranking-and-questions.md`.

---

## Guardrails for Path B

- Do not rank before cleaning — the order matters. An unformatted hypothesis list produces
  a distorted ranking because the founder can't compare apples to apples.
- Never discard a hypothesis without telling the founder. Either rewrite it or ask them to.
- If a hypothesis is solution-focused, rewrite it as a belief about customer behavior or the
  problem — not as an assertion about what the product does.
- One cleanup conversation at a time. Don't show a revised list and ask for ranking feedback
  simultaneously — confirm the list first, rank second.
