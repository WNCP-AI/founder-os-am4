Vertical analytics: structured expert review with a domain specialist evaluating the analytical output. Data tools: feedback mechanism must be distinct from the data interface being tested — observation script or structured expert review with a domain data lead. Marketplace: cover both supply and demand sides, or explicitly pick one and flag the other as a Day 3+ gap.
