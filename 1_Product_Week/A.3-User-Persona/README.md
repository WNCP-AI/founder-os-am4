# A.3-User-Persona

Mon Jun 15 · 11:00–11:30 AM (shared with A.3-Value-Prop) · In-person — Valerius Living, Denver · ~15m instruction + workshop.

Converge on one laser-focused persona — the **A (Analyze + Architect)** move of CRAFT+ for people. Value prop runs first and anchors who is in scope; this half pins down exactly who that person is. The output narrows the JTBD space before A.4.

**The beats — one persona, full stop.** Your product may be multifaceted, but this exercise is not — a persona range is no persona. Name a role, not a demographic band: "mothers" tells you what they care about, "women aged 25–35" does not. Distinguish user from customer — if they are different people, both need personas (Uber's driver vs. rider split is the model). Acquisition channel belongs here too — where this persona discovers products is part of the persona, not marketing. Validate the persona against your customer-interview data, not invented from assumption.

**What you do.** Open `A.3-User-Persona-Template.md` — seven fields: Identity, Situation, Need, Current behavior, Key quote, Why this ICP, Out of scope. The `user-persona` skill reads your A.2 discovery data first, then coaches Identity → Situation → Need; Current behavior and Key quote are surfaced from the discovery files if present. The three validation criteria: one person (no range), a specific moment (not a general state), a solution-agnostic need (no product name in the sentence).

**Where work goes.** You complete the template by hand and it stays in this folder; the agent's derived markdown deliverable lands in `2_Outputs/.agents/A.3-User-Persona/User-Persona.md`; the slimmed essence folds into `0_Company/`.

The skill blocks two-persona attempts with a hard stop. "Out of scope" is a required field — plausible user types explicitly ruled out are as important as the persona chosen. Marketplace founders: declare which side you are writing from at the start; flag the other side in Out of scope. The persona is upstream of JTBD — if the persona is vague, A.4 starves.
