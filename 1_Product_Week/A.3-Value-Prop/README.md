# A.3-Value-Prop

Mon Jun 15 · 11:00–11:30 AM (shared with A.3-User-Persona) · In-person — Valerius Living, Denver · 10m teach / 10m workshop.

The tightest talk of the week — 10 minutes of instruction — lands one idea: the value prop is not a pitch; it is a solution-agnostic sentence specific enough to drive a real sale.

**The beat — the value prop is a sale test, not a pitch.** Specificity is what drives a real sale, not polish. What you want over everything is ravenous fans — people who are upset and blowing up your phone every time your server goes down. "Nothing" is your most-forgotten competitor: a value prop has to clear the bar of solving the problem at all. Build the sentence from problem, not product: **[Product] helps [person] who [problem], by [solution], unlike [alternative]**.

**What you do.** Open `A.3-Value-Prop-Template.md` and start the `4-value-proposition` skill. The skill asks three questions (Problem → For Whom → Alternative); the product stays out of the sentence until Phase 3. If you already have a value prop, say so at the start — the skill switches to Mode B and probes the four criteria one at a time. You write the sentence; the skill will not generate it for you.

**Where work goes.** You complete the template by hand and it stays in this folder; the agent's derived markdown deliverable lands in `2_Outputs/.agents/A.3-Value-Prop/Value-Proposition.md`; the slimmed essence folds into `0_Company/`.

The skill halts if no A.2 discovery data exists in `sources/processed/`. The value prop may change — B-block 1:1s may revise it; the template output is a mutable stake in the ground, not a contract. Marketplace founders: pick one side for this session.
