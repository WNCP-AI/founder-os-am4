# A.3-Value-Prop

Mon Jun 15 · 11:00–11:30 AM · In-person

In the first half of the A.3 slot, founders leave with one sentence: who the customer is, what problem they have, what the product does about it, and what the alternative to doing nothing looks like. The output is a one-sentence value prop plus a four-field structured block that narrows the JTBD space before A.4 starts.

This folder is the in-session working space. You complete the template **by hand** and it stays here; the agent's derived markdown deliverable goes to `2_Outputs/.agents/<session>/`. Session plan and naming convention: `0_System/spec/Founder OS PRD v1.md`, §A.1.
