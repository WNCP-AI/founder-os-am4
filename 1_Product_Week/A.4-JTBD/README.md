# A.4-JTBD

Mon Jun 15 · 11:30 AM–12:30 PM · In-person — Valerius Living, Denver · 20m teach / 40m workshop.

The pivot of Day 1: discovery becomes a prioritized problem the PRD is built on. Value prop and persona in; a ranked job out; top job straight into A.5's PRD. Jobs are written at the **feature level** and kept solution-agnostic; market research and metrics are deferred to later sessions.

**The beats — what makes a valid job.** A job is a need or pain your customer hires a product to solve — start from the customer's progress, not your product. Define jobs at the feature level, not the app level: not "I need a maps app" but "I need a way to determine the best route to work before I leave the house." "Nothing" is your most-forgotten competitor — pull out the workarounds the customer has already settled for. Name the riskiest assumption behind each job (Uber's: people will get into an unlicensed driver's car) — the biggest ones become a criterion when you prioritize. **Make the AI-role call per job — this is the WNCP move**: one call per job (Feature / Product core / Not yet / Irrelevant) plus one line of why, never one blanket "AI-first." Prioritize to one north-star, then rank and kill — anchor to one focus the business needs now: growth, engagement, retention, or monetization.

**What you do.** Write 3–5 statements by hand in the JTBD template in *When [situation] / I want to [goal] / So I can [outcome]* form; tag each job's primary layer (functional, emotional, or social) and make its AI-role call with a one-line rationale. Chain into `jtbd-prioritizer` — answer the five-dimension questions conversationally (pain intensity · existing alternatives · strategic fit · founder edge · evidence quality), let it rank, then record a keep-or-kill on every non-top job and the riskiest assumption on each survivor. Pull the workarounds your customers already use out of your discovery notes — they are feature input, not decoration.

**Where work goes.** You complete `A.4-JTBD-Template.md` by hand and it stays in this folder; the agent's derived markdown deliverables land in `2_Outputs/.agents/A.4-JTBD/JTBD.md` and `JTBD-Prioritized.md`; the slimmed essence folds into `0_Company/`.

A valid statement has no product in it — if the sentence collapses when you remove the solution, it was never a job. A kill isn't a parking lot — it's what's left when a job doesn't survive the three checks. The top job, not the loudest job, anchors PRD §1.
