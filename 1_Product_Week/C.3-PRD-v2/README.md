# C.3-PRD-v2

Wed Jun 17 · 1:00–3:00 PM (shared with C.3-Prototyping-2) · In-person — Valerius Living, Denver · 15m teach / 1h 45m workshop · hard stop 3:00 PM.

PRD v2 is a *new* file — not an extension of v1. The 1:1s may have already shifted the value prop, so v2 is rebuilt fresh from B-day 1:1 feedback, C.2 OKRs, and the C.3 Peer Court triage, against the restructured 9-section template. AI Clinic runs opt-in alongside the workshop for anyone whose product lives inside the AI stack.

**The beats — triage, then refine, not rewrite.** v2 is a new file; v1 is kept as the snapshot. Triage Peer Court to the single highest-signal change — the one that most tests the riskiest assumption. "Highest-signal change" and "riskiest assumption" should be the same thing; if they aren't, you triaged wrong. Refine the change, not the whole product — keep it testable. **§7 Assumptions & Risks** call out the unvalidated bet (e.g., users will get into an unlicensed driver's car). **§8 OKRs drop in from C.2** — already formatted, decision-gated, ambition-checked; do not redo them. **§9 Roadmap** is the sequencing out from the highest-signal change.

**AI Clinic (opt-in).** Commoditization risk: who can launch your product tomorrow — Perplexity in search, Midjourney in image gen. Model types have different risk profiles: LLMs vs. vision vs. diffusion vs. voice vs. video. Case studies: Harvey (legal) vs. Anthropic's legal model — what happens when the model vendor eats your category. The first question: who are you selling to — end customers or AI platform audiences? The answer changes the risk.

**What you do.** Read your Peer Court notes; pick the single highest-signal change. Open the v2 template and write §7 (assumptions & risks), §8 (OKRs from C.2), and §9 (roadmap) by hand; §5–§6 carry forward from v1. For each riskiest assumption in §7, jot one line on how you'll validate it before Friday. The agent helps draft, but you write the sentences.

**Where work goes.** `C.3-PRD-v2-Template.md` stays in this folder as the founder's hand-completed work; the agent's derived deliverable lands in `2_Outputs/.agents/C.3-PRD-v2/PRD-v2.md`; the slimmed essence folds into `0_Company/`.

**Hard stop at 3:00 PM** — unfinished work carries to Thursday AM, not into the Fireside Chat. The PRD skill must be Socratic — the agent asks, the founder writes. If the skill is auto-generating the PRD, it's broken.
