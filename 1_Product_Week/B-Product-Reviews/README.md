# B-Product-Reviews

Tue Jun 16 · 10:00–12:30 + 1:30–3:30 PM · In-person (optionally remote)

Two blocks of 1:1 product reviews (B.1 × 5 founders, B.2 × 4 founders, 30 minutes each). The founder owns the agenda and brings the PRD and the prototype; the 25-minute working review focuses on defensibility, the riskiest assumption, and scope, and closes with concrete next actions for Wednesday's Peer Court and PRD v2 work.

This folder is the in-session working space. You complete the template **by hand** and it stays here; the agent's derived markdown deliverable goes to `2_Outputs/.agents/<session>/`. Session plan and naming convention: `0_System/spec/Founder OS PRD v1.md`, §A.1.
