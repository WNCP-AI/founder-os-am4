# B-Product-Reviews

Tue Jun 16 · In-person (optionally remote) · 30m per founder · Block 1: 10:00 AM–12:30 PM (×5) · Block 2: 1:30–3:30 PM (×4).

Two blocks of 1:1 product reviews. The 1:1 is where the value prop gets refined (not just polished) and the v2 PRD gets seeded, especially for ideas that shifted during the review. It feeds Peer Court and the v2 PRD.

**The beat — the founder owns the agenda.** The 25-minute working review focuses on the riskiest assumption and scope. Cover venture vs. non-venture: scale must be planned from day one for venture-backed founders; bootstrapped founders optimize for early profitability — this framing changes what the PRD prioritizes.

**What you do.** Bring your PRD and your prototype. 5m setup — walk the facilitator through your current PRD and prototype state. 25m working review on the riskiest assumption and scope. Open office hours follow Tue 3:30–4:00 PM for drop-in follow-ups or build alongside the cohort.

**Where work goes.** No fixed output path — founder leaves with a sharpened riskiest assumption and concrete next actions for Wednesday's Peer Court and PRD v2 work. Value props may genuinely change here, not just be polished — that's expected and healthy.
