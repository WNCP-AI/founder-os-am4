# C.2-Metrics-OKR

Wed Jun 17 · 11:00 AM–12:00 PM · In-person — Valerius Living, Denver · 20m teach / 40m workshop.

One-hour Metrics & OKR Workshop. Founders write **2 Objectives × 2–3 Key Results each** as validation signals for the riskiest assumption in their PRD — not activity counts.

**The beats — KRs validate the bet, not the busy.** KRs must validate the riskiest assumption — not DAUs, sessions, or feature counts. "Did the bet pay off, or were you just busy?" is the self-check. Ambition check: target a 50–60% hit rate, not 100%. Map each KR to a decision: **Ship / Pivot / Tweak / Kill.** OKRs ladder to one of: growth, engagement, retention, or monetization — pick the one the business needs now (Damien's Facebook 6-of-7-days stickiness is the engagement example). Objectives do not need to be purely numeric — "reduce user time on X" or "increase trust perception" are valid.

**What you do.** Open `C.2-Metrics-OKR-Template.md` and draft 2 objectives × 2–3 KRs each. Map each KR to a decision gate. Confirm the format lands directly in PRD v2 §8 (OKRs) — it is pre-formatted, no rework.

**Where work goes.** You complete the template by hand and it stays in this folder; the agent's derived markdown deliverable lands in `2_Outputs/.agents/C.2-Metrics-OKR/Metrics-OKR.md`; the slimmed essence folds into `0_Company/`.
