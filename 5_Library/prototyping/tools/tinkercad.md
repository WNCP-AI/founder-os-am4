# Tinkercad

Free browser-based 3D design and circuit simulation tool by Autodesk — build a physical product mockup or basic circuit without any CAD experience.

- **Pricing:** Free. No credit card required.

**5-Step Quickstart**
1. Go to tinkercad.com and sign up with email or Google (free Autodesk account)
2. Click 'Create new design' under '3D Designs'
3. Drag and drop basic shapes from the right panel onto the canvas — combine them to approximate your product's physical form
4. Use the Group tool to merge shapes, and the Align tool to position them precisely — zoom in to refine details
5. Click 'Export' to download as STL for 3D printing, or take a screenshot of the model to include in your spec deck
