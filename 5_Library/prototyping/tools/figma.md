# Figma

Browser-based design tool for building interactive wireframes and high-fidelity mockups — no code required. Share a clickable prototype link before writing a single line of code.

- **Pricing:** Free Starter plan (unlimited drafts, 3 Figma files). Professional from $15/month.

**5-Step Quickstart**
1. Go to figma.com and sign up with Google or email — the Starter plan is free, no credit card needed
2. Click 'New design file' from the home screen to open the canvas
3. Press F to open the Frame tool — pick a device size (e.g. iPhone 14) to set your canvas, then add shapes, text, and images to design your screens
4. Use the community templates (search in Resources → Community) to skip the blank canvas — find a UI kit and customize it
5. Click the 'Prototype' tab, drag connectors between screens to link them, then hit 'Present' to get a shareable clickable prototype link
